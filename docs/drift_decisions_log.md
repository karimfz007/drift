**D-117 · 2026-08-04 — DROP 3, PART 2: CONSTRUCTION II. Shelter quality stops being one number — and the LDOE bar finally has a definition it can FAIL.**

**THE BAR WAS INVOKED FOR FOUR CYCLES AND NEVER DEFINED.** [[D-040]] closed Cycle 03 with *"systems pass, feel fails the LDOE bar"*; [[D-069]] folded it into the construction slice's spine. In all that time it named no property, so it could be cited but never met — *"make it feel as good as the reference"* is not a specification, and a bar you cannot fail is not a bar. **The five properties are the DIRECTOR'S, given when this item asked for them**, kept close to verbatim in `PLACEMENT_BAR` so a later reading cannot drift the intent. C2 supplied one number (`placementReadableFromM`, 12 m) and the decision about which layer can honestly witness each property. Both are named as the first things to move at playtest.

**THE HONEST HALF OF THE BAR IS THAT TWO PROPERTIES ARE NOT UNIT-TESTABLE.** The ghost and the one-tap commit are claims about a GESTURE, and no unit test can witness them. They are declared `witness: 'device'` and the declaration is itself asserted — a test fails if anyone quietly reclassifies them as brain-side. **A bar whose properties are all unit-testable has excluded the feel it exists to measure**, which is how this one stayed unfalsifiable for four cycles.

**PROPERTY 3 IS THE ONE WORTH THE MODULE.** *"No walk up, get told no, walk somewhere else."* The checkable form is not "is there a readout at range" — any UI can fake that. It is that **the verdict computed from 12 m away is IDENTICAL to the verdict standing on the spot**. If those can differ, the walk is load-bearing and the loop exists no matter what the screen shows on approach. Swept over 400 states, with a vacuity guard asserting the sweep actually produced blocked sites rather than proving `true === true` on 400 valid ones.

**THE VULNERABILITY MAP: FOUR THREATS, ANSWERED SEPARATELY.** Wind, rain, cold and ground damp. A lean-to is a roof and does nothing about the cold coming up through your back; a cave is the best roof on the island and the worst floor. **Neither is better — they fail differently**, and a survivor who knows which is which can fix the half that is failing them. `thermal.ts` already had a named term per threat because [[D-092]] built it that way; what was missing was a shelter able to speak to them one at a time. **This module produces COEFFICIENTS and the shipped resolver keeps every equation** — there is no second calculation to drift.

**F3'S CERTIFIED BAND IS PRESERVED BY CONSTRUCTION — AND IT STILL CAUGHT ME.** The built shelter's profile is defined as `1 - shelterGradeWarmthMultiplier[grade]` on exactly the two threats the retired flat factor covered, and zero on the two it never touched, so it reproduces the shipped arithmetic term for term (asserted to ten decimal places at every grade). Then I wired `activeProfile` to read `state.shelter.built` — and **F3's own certified test broke inside one run**, along with C05's "disrepair pauses the bonus" rule, because a survivor half the island away from a ruined shelter was getting its full protection. **Being BUILT is not being SHELTERED.** `reconcile` already computes `shelterActive` as built AND near AND sound; the function now takes that answer rather than guessing it again. *Two sources for one fact is the exact drift this module was written to prevent, and I introduced it anyway on the first pass.*

**THE CAVE IS A TRADE, NOT AN UPGRADE**, and it is the reason the map earns its keep. Rain fully answered, wind nearly, cold well but short of a shelter you worked for — and `groundDamp` **NEGATIVE**: bare cave floor conducts 1.5x open ground. Move in without bedding and you have traded a windy night for a colder back. Dry bedding still fixes it, so the survivor's existing answer keeps working, which is what stops it being a punishment. No number in the game could have said that before this pass, because one number cannot be good and bad at once.

**IT IS TERRAIN, SO IT MERGES ON MIGRATION — BUT UNFOUND.** v21 → v22. Illness migrated in fresh because a condition is a fact about a body we have no record of; geology is the opposite, and a save that never gains the cave is a save on a different island ([[D-112]]'s shape, for [[D-112]]'s reason). The rock is real whether or not anyone has seen it; **the KNOWING is the survivor's**, and handing it over would skip the only part that is theirs. Found and sheltering are separate fields because a survivor who found the cave in daylight should not unlearn it by walking out.

**D-116 · 2026-08-04 — DROP 3, PART 1: THE MEDICINE SLICE. Illness is CAUSED, never rolled — and three of the brief's premises did not exist.**

**THE PREMISES, NAMED BEFORE ANYTHING WAS BUILT ON THEM.** The slice arrived described as "fully speced already (register, body-model trunk)". Three of its load-bearing references are absent from this repository: **the immunity ladder** (zero occurrences in `src/` or `docs/`), **the food-safety law** (not in the living docs), and **the Medicine Slice's register rows** — [[D-078]]'s Ch.10 names and sequences the slice, and [[D-069]] records v0_13 as *"file pending — register rows to follow once it arrives"*. Built rather than blocked, under stated assumptions, and every design decision below is C2's rather than a transcription.

**`illness.ts` MIRRORS `injury.ts` FUNCTION FOR FUNCTION**, so the second condition system reads as a sibling of the first rather than a rival to it. It differs in exactly two places and both are stated in its header.

**DIFFERENCE 1 — CAUSED, NEVER ROLLED.** There is no random source anywhere in the module. `onsetFrom` is the only way an illness can begin and it demands an explicit cause; the cause **sticks to the first one**, so a chill that deepens a water-borne illness cannot rewrite where it came from; and `illnessNote` names it at every rung. A readout the player cannot trace would make illness arbitrary in precisely the way the design forbids.

**DIFFERENCE 2 — ABSENCE HOLDS IT RATHER THAN CLEARING IT.** `settleInjuriesOffline` clots everything, because a bleed is acute. Illness cannot take that rule: a free cure for closing the game would make closing the game the best medicine on the island, and the whole slice optional. So the absence rule is the weaker, truer one — **never worse, never costly**, with the grave cases eased to `illnessOfflineCeiling` (0.4), deliberately BELOW `illnessFeverishAt` (0.45) so a survivor who closes at death's door does not open there. [[D-011]] is satisfied structurally, exactly as injury satisfies it: `stepIllness` is the only function that returns `healthLost`, and reconcile's absence path cannot reach it.

**THE FIVE-STAGE GRAMMAR TELLS YOU TWICE BEFORE IT BITES.** `unsettled` and `ailing` cost nothing and both say something; health starts moving only at `feverish`, which is 45% of the range. The fair-challenge contract is held in ONE predicate — `illnessCosts` — so callers cannot re-derive it and a retune cannot quietly move the line.

**THE RESISTANCE LADDER IS DERIVED, NOT STORED.** The brief's "immunity ladder" is `susceptibility()`: the worst of fatigue, wounding, cold and starvation, read from state the game already keeps. **There is no `immunity` field** to migrate, to desync, or to forget to update — the same condition that makes you vulnerable to bad water makes you vulnerable to a chill, because it is the same survivor. Floor is 0.25: capable, never immune, which is the charter's own line.

**ITEM 4 REUSES THE SHIPPED REST MODEL RATHER THAN ADDING A SECOND.** `restQuality` is the exact term reconcile already uses to recover energy — 1 sheltered, `groundSleepRecoveryMultiplier` (0.55) rough, 0 awake. A warm dry bed heals an illness faster **because it is already the better bed**, in the number the game already speaks.

**ITEM 5 — FORAGING & MEDICINE FINALLY HAS A PRODUCER.** Seven domains have existed since [[D-056]] and this one has sat at its innate floor the entire time, because nothing ever asked the survivor to know anything about it. `brewRemedy` trains it. The remedy is the **fifth verb on the fire** — the load the radial circle exists to carry, which a precedence order could not arbitrate — costs an hour through `spendGameHours`, and **relieves rather than cures**, because the recovery clock is the system and a one-tap cure would delete it.

**SPOILAGE REUSES LAW 128 RATHER THAN ADDING A CLOCK.** Berries and meat already transform to `contaminated` on a failed attempt and `matterWear` already counts how far gone a kind is, so the food that can make you ill is exactly the food the game has been describing as crushed and spoiled. Nothing new is stored, and the warning was on screen before the bite. The **carried flask is deliberately not a cause** — it is what you filled and carried; one water source with a cost and one without is the decision.

**DROPPED ITEMS, AND [[D-011]] READ THE ONLY WAY THAT GUARANTEES IT (item 2).** Each stack carries its **own** `droppedAtGameHours`. Picking one up removes its entry entirely, so dropping again mints a new one with a new timestamp — *"resets its own timer" is structural*, with no separate reset call for anyone to forget. The instruction was "hygiene, never an offline-harm vector", and **the only way to guarantee that is for the timer not to run while the game is closed AT ALL** — `pruneDropped` is called from the online tick only, and `dropped.ts` exposes no absence-path counterpart, the same structural shape as the boars and the injuries, asserted by a test that greps the module's own exports. Schema **v19 → v20**.

**THE WOUND HAS A PLACE TO BE ASKED ABOUT (item 3).** The HUD note is one sentence that appears and passes; the Vitals tab is where a survivor comes to ask *how bad is it, actually*. Bleeding, limp and pain each report in their own units, because they are three different problems with three different answers — a bind, a wait, and a cost on everything you do. **L/R equip reuses the shipped mechanism**: `equipToSupportHand` mirrors `equipToActiveHand`'s rules rather than inventing a second set, adding only the two refusals specific to a second hand — a two-handed tool cannot go there, and nothing can while the active hand holds something two-handed. The same physical fact from both directions, which is the whole reason `supportHand` is modelled at all. Refusals speak in nearest-true-reason, like the circle.

**THE SKILLS TAB SHOWS THE SKILLS (item 4).** Audit first, per instruction: the tab carried §12's eight capacities and §15's three crossings — both real, both about what the island has done to the **body**, and neither of them a skill. `woodcutting` and `foraging` have shipped since Cycle 03 with levels that drive real speed multipliers, and a survivor had nowhere to see either one, so *"am I getting better at this"* was answerable only by feel. Both now read **above** the capacities: a skill is what the player is deliberately practising, the capacities are what happens to them while they do it. Sleeping is also never *worse* than being awake in the same spot: the warmth rate takes `Math.max` of the resting rate and the awake rate, so dozing by a roaring fire still gets the fire's much better rate.
3. **Fatigue.** A 0–100 value accruing while ONLINE and in energy debt, shed by rest, read out in three perceivable stages (mild / moderate / severe) with plain status text. **Deliberately absent from the health-drain path entirely** — that is what keeps D-011's offline-death-impossible law structurally intact with a new body state added, the same scope-out energy's own C05 introduction made.
4. **The death cost + lesson.** A death now takes a floored `deathResourceLossFraction` (0.25) of each CARRIED loose resource stack — **and nothing else**. Tools survive, stored goods survive, skills survive, and `KnowledgeState` survives: Ch.2's amendment B is a standing law, and this is simply the second system that has to respect it. `Math.floor` means a stack of 1–3 loses nothing, so rounding never wipes a small stack and the poorest castaway is not punished hardest. **Two judgment calls, stated rather than buried:** (a) the fraction — 0.25 against a realistic 10–20 units carried costs 2–5 units, versus one felled tree yielding 8 wood, so it is minutes of play to recover, a real sting that is never a setback spiral; (b) **"loose/uncarried" was read as CARRIED loose stacks, and storage was deliberately left untouched** — what you were carrying scatters where you fell, what you took the trouble to store at home is exactly the investment a built crate is meant to protect. The ruling's phrasing admits a second reading (storage) and this one is flagged for cheap correction if C1 meant otherwise. Each death also records a cause-specific lesson message and exactly what it cost, in the death log.

**Save schema bumped v7→v8** (`migrateV7toV8`): `fatigue` and `resting` are new; a returning player wakes with zero fatigue and not resting — the honest "we have no record of how tired you were" answer, and the one this chapter's own offline law demands (inventing a fatigue number for a save that predates the system would be absence making the body worse).
