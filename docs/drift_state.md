A pure evaluator scores five factors (challenge/novelty/feedback/consequence/reflection) into a (Technique, Understanding) delta that shrinks toward zero as a domain nears mastery, by construction, never a special case. - **Effort has a cost (D-052).** Every effortful (hold-interaction) gather — a felled tree, a mined rock or quarry tap, a deadfall salvage, a coconut palm, forcing the crash box — now spends a tuned amount of energy on top of the ambient per-game-hour drain; a plain tap (driftwood, berries, reeds, shellfish, salvage) costs nothing extra. - **Death is a setback, not a free refill (D-052, interim).** Respawn now wakes thirst/hunger/energy at half of max and health lower still, instead of topping every vital to full — warmth is the one deliberate exception (the acute killer, Rule of Threes).
