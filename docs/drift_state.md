## What exists now (the inheritance every later cycle gets free)
- **The island is no longer the world ([[D-121]], the Maritime Slice, brain-layer + an unwitnessed body half).** The 108 m clamp is deleted and the seabed shelves, so dry / wading / swimming are read from `waterDepthAt` (= `seaLevel − groundHeight`) and there is **no water-radius constant anywhere**; `SURF_LINE_RADIUS` is now SOLVED from the terrain by bisection, keeping [[D-064]]'s law — *the thing you see is the thing that acts on you* — while replacing its mechanism. Cold-water immersion is `wet` at `wetMax` and reaches warmth through the SHIPPED heat balance: **no second thermal system**. Every swim cost lives on the ONLINE tick and nowhere else, so **[[D-011]] holds structurally** — `reconcile` has no swim cost to floor, and no rescue-teleport exists to make sure of it. Five stages (wading / swimming / labouring / spent / going-under) put **three stages and two spoken warnings before health is ever touched**, and the stage that takes it is §12's `unsafe-continued`. `breathWaterConfidence`, `endurance`, `mobilityBalance` and `navigationSeamanship` all get their **first real producers** — `developCapacity` had shipped at [[D-089]] with zero callers, so eight capacities existed and none could move. The **raft** is the first built thing in this game that MOVES: earned through the discovery spine, gated by materials AND by standing at the water, it carries the survivor, grounds rather than driving up the beach, and survives a death as matter with `aboard` forced false. The **crossing** to the wreck is ~115 m of open water, tuned so a full reserve gets you there and not back — the raft is the answer, asserted as arithmetic in two tests so a later tune cannot quietly make it optional. Save schema **v22 → v23**. **936/936 unit; no device leg — see the owed item above.**
- **The body has weight, tiredness, and a real cost of dying (D-058, Ch.6, brain-layer).** Carried materials and owned tools have mass; the total sorts into three load bands that slow movement and raise energy cost (multiplying the existing D-052 rates, never a parallel system — `light` is exactly 1, so nothing changes for an empty pack). Sleeping is a recovery RATE over elapsed hours, not the instant refill it was through C05 — bounded, and never worse than being awake in the same spot.
