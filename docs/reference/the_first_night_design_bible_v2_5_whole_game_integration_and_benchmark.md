# THE FIRST NIGHT — ONE GAME, NOT A LIBRARY OF IDEAS
## Design Bible v2.5 — Whole-Game Integration, Quantitative Spine, and Competitive Benchmark Constitution

**Project:** THE FIRST NIGHT / DRIFT / RUSTED  
**Date:** 31 July 2026  
**Authority:** Whole-game integration and production-audit layer over Design Bible v2.2–v2.4 and specialist constitutions v0.7–v0.15. It does not silently repeal a more specific physical law.  
**Implementation boundary:** No feature expansion is authorized before the v0.1.2 real-hardware interaction recovery gate passes.  
**Companion model:** `the_first_night_whole_game_integration_model_v2_5.xlsx`

---

# 1. Director answer

I have a clear image of the game.

The concept no longer needs another unrestricted brainstorming discussion. Its
center is now strong and distinct:

> **A wounded castaway stabilizes a life, learns civilization through physical
> evidence and experiment, turns a changing wreck-fed island into an inherited
> home, undertakes increasingly capable expeditions across land, sea and depth,
> and eventually earns a truthful choice between return, community and a home
> made on the island.**

The present weakness is not lack of imagination. It is integration.

The corpus contains sophisticated treatments of survival, fire, shelter,
invention, tools, wrecks, boats, diving, animals, cultivation, death, legacy,
Community Island and return. Most are individually persuasive. They are not yet
one calculable, testable machine.

The next work must therefore be:

1. reconcile every feature into one authoritative system registry;
2. express dependencies as a machine-testable directed hypergraph;
3. give every activity one shared body–time–matter resolution contract;
4. calculate resource, labor, travel, risk and campaign budgets;
5. benchmark both the designed game and the actual build against category
   leaders;
6. prototype representative loops until the player’s hands prove the design;
7. bank any idea that cannot demonstrate its role in the playable spine.

Only a small number of genuine Director decisions remain. They should be raised
after the models expose real alternatives, not used to delay the audit.

# 2. Fresh-eye verdict

## 2.1 What is already exceptional

THE FIRST NIGHT has six concepts with genuine signature potential:

1. **Nothing is a recipe until someone proves it.**  
   Materials expose properties; attempts expose relations; testing establishes
   patterns; physical records carry knowledge.

2. **Civilization grows through ancestry.**  
   An engine repair, radio, seaworthy boat or fortified home cannot escape the
   tools, fixtures, processed forms, measurements, handling and prior
   capabilities beneath it.

3. **Wrecks are closing windows of work.**  
   They flood, settle, burn, corrode, become obscured, change routes and create
   environmental obligations. They are not loot boxes.

4. **The island remembers through matter.**  
   Death ends a person. The next castaway inherits structures, condition,
   hazards, archives, tools, gardens, unfinished work and the consequences of
   earlier judgment—not magical account knowledge.

5. **The home is accumulated promises made physical.**  
   Shelter does not grant a bundle of buffs. Roof, dry interface, wind control,
   drainage, fire, storage, water, cultivation and protection each solve a
   specific path and create a specific obligation.

6. **Escape is not yet home.**  
   Detection, authentication, receiving commitment, boundary transfer,
   reception, identity, onward transit and Human Thread resolution distinguish
   mere departure from true return.

These should be protected. They are more valuable than another hundred items,
animals or death scenarios.

## 2.2 What can still make the game fail

The same project can become:

- a slow first-person menu simulator;
- an opaque random-combination game;
- a stack of realistic meters that multiply into unwinnable collapse;
- an enormous technology catalogue with no emotionally coherent campaign;
- a beautiful design document whose build never reaches its promise;
- a mobile interface carrying PC-scale complexity;
- a permanent-death game that repeats chores rather than producing history;
- a home-building game that makes leaving irrational;
- a return-home game that postpones its ending until production can never reach
  it.

The answer is not simplification by removing the soul. The answer is
**compression through shared laws**.

# 3. The missing master layer

Every implemented system must receive one System Contract.

| Contract field | Required question |
|---|---|
| Player verb | What does the player physically do? |
| Preconditions | What matter, tool, workspace, body condition, knowledge and place make the attempt possible? |
| State read | What can the survivor actually perceive before committing? |
| Resolution | Which shared calculation changes body, time, matter, environment and knowledge? |
| Feedback | What is seen, heard, felt or recorded immediately? |
| Failure ladder | How can the player abort, stabilize, change method, accept smaller loss or recover? |
| Output | What object, condition, evidence, route, relationship or capability changes? |
| Downstream use | Which later decision becomes possible or different? |
| Campaign role | Why does this belong between arrival and a chosen ending? |
| Persistence | What survives saving, absence, death and successor arrival? |
| Acceptance | What human behavior proves the system is understandable and enjoyable? |

A feature without one of these fields is not ready for production.

## 3.1 Orphan rule

An idea is an orphan when it:

- consumes resources but enables no meaningful future choice;
- changes a meter but produces no readable behavior;
- exists only to support another unimplemented catalogue;
- has no recovery path;
- is available but not discoverable;
- is discoverable but never useful;
- repeats an earlier verb without a new decision;
- is disconnected from home, expedition, inheritance or return.

Orphans are banked, linked or cut. They are never implemented merely because
they appear in a prior document.

## 3.2 One source of production truth

Every feature must carry five independent states:

| Axis | Allowed states |
|---|---|
| Canon | ABSENT · PROPOSED · RULED · SPECIFIED |
| Build | ABSENT · HOOK · PROTOTYPE · INTEGRATED |
| Automated evidence | NONE · UNIT · SCENE · END-TO-END |
| Human evidence | NONE · DESKTOP · WEB-TOUCH · ANDROID-DEVICE |
| Release | INTERNAL · TEST · PRERELEASE · RELEASED |

No single word such as **SHIPPED** may replace this record.

# 4. The whole-game playable spine

The game should be authored and balanced around one complete road before its
branches expand.

| Phase | Player problem | New or deepened verb | Permanent change | Dominant pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Translation | Get out of immediate danger while compromised | orient, free, wade/crawl, inspect | first body evidence and personal object | airway, surf, smoke or impact |
| 1. Safe pocket | Stop deterioration and understand the body | cover, wring, rest, bind, relocate | readable condition and first local map | wetness, wind, pain |
| 2. First relation | Turn loose matter into controlled action | inspect, stage, strike, bind, test | first demonstrated tool relation | time and failing light |
| 3. Fire discovery | Produce and sustain useful heat without a recipe | prepare, ignite, feed, shield, diagnose | fire evidence and a tentative pattern | cold, wet fuel, dexterity |
| 4. Truthful refuge | Combine roof, dry interface and wind control | site, carry, brace, place, correct | first attachment to place | material commitment |
| 5. Water Run | Leave safety to establish hydration continuity | locate, assess, contain, carry, treat | home gains a water operation | distance, contamination, load |
| 6. Wreck Window | Read and act on a changing opportunity | observe, plan, stabilize, access, detach | recovered capability-changing object | tide, flooding, weather, injury |
| 7. Maker Lab | Convert evidence into repeatable capability | compare, measure, jig, vary, document | dependable patterns and archive | precision, scarcity, attention |
| 8. Working home | Make safety productive but costly | store, repair, dry, preserve, cultivate | routes and obligations accumulate | maintenance and logistics |
| 9. Fishing boat B0–B3 | Turn a stranded object into a useful machine | secure, patch, launch, load, paddle | floating/paddled workboat | flotation, balance, exposure |
| 10. Sea and depth | Extend the island’s reachable world | navigate, reboard, dive, recover, tow | new materials, evidence and routes | weather, air, return reserve |
| 11. Return Program | Choose and build a credible way outward | authenticate, repair, test, provision | one actionable return hypothesis | proof, compatibility, timing |
| 12. Boundary operation | Leave without reducing departure to a cutscene | launch, transmit, cross, rendezvous | external commitment and island consequence | navigation and irreversibility |
| 13. Reception | Survive the world beyond the anomaly | identify, explain, submit evidence, travel | recognized person and resolved Human Thread | credibility, time, jurisdiction |
| 14. Chosen ending | Decide what home now means | return, remain, rejoin or prepare succession | campaign statement | attachment, obligation, loss |

This spine is not a tutorial checklist. It is the first proof route against
which every system is tested.

# 5. Twenty-six whole-game weak points

## P0 — Can invalidate the game

### G01 — Canon is rich but fragmented

More than 70,000 words of recent specialist canon overlap. Later documents
correct earlier ones, but there is no clause-level reconciliation register and
no machine-readable feature state.

**Strengthening:** maintain one System Registry and Decision Conflict Log.
Specialist documents retain depth; the registry owns current status, system
owner and governing clause.

### G02 — No central state resolver owns causal multiplication

Warmth, wetness, injury, load, fatigue, hunger, thirst, energy, tool condition,
skill, weather and time are described together but not resolved through one
authoritative calculation.

**Failure:** independent multipliers create sudden death spirals, double-charge
the same cause or allow sleep/fire/shelter to fabricate recovery.

**Strengthening:** every activity submits one declaration to a shared resolver.
The resolver applies immediate, delayed and recovery-phase changes once.

### G03 — The capability graph is not executable

The 44 endpoint families in v2.3 have strong ancestry, but they are not stored
as a dataset that can prove reachability, detect circular dependencies, measure
bottlenecks or show whether one lost resource makes return impossible.

**Strengthening:** encode nodes and edges; run automated checks for:

- unreachable required nodes;
- dependencies on future outputs;
- single-source critical materials;
- missing alternative methods;
- unused outputs;
- gates with no discoverable evidence;
- endpoints without qualification tests.

### G04 — The game has no built complete campaign

Borrowed Signal is authored conceptually, but the code proves only:

> gather → static recipe craft → menu building → campfire → warmth → save

The promise of invention, expedition, succession and return has never been
experienced end to end.

**Strengthening:** build one compressed but emotionally complete road before
expanding content.

### G05 — The hands are still proof-of-concept

Movement, look, collecting, tool contact, placement and touch controls received
repair work, but the Director’s physical test invalidated automated usability
acceptance. Current visuals remain primitives and several interaction surfaces
overlap or become unreadable on mobile.

**Strengthening:** an Embodied Play Lab must prove:

- 30 seconds of movement are pleasant without objectives;
- targeting is stable while moving;
- no click or modal can latch control;
- tool contact has anticipation, impact, material response and recovery;
- carrying mass feels different without becoming input latency;
- building correction is faster than building error;
- Android controls remain legible during every context.

### G06 — Empty-book invention lacks a finished discovery language

The ruling is excellent; the risk is severe. If unknown objects reveal no
recipes, the world must reveal **affordances**:

- stone edge and fracture;
- fiber length and twist;
- wood dryness and grain;
- part motion and fastening points;
- heat, spark, friction and smoke;
- buoyancy, leakage and balance;
- evidence left by failed operations.

**Strengthening:** the combination interface should stage **relations**, not
guess outputs. The player chooses an intention such as cut, bind, contain,
support, ignite, seal, turn or transmit; material properties and context make
some operations plausible. Unknown outputs stay unknown.

### G07 — Quantities exist without a calibration constitution

Some early values are explicit, but most advanced activity, salvage, ecology,
tool quality, boat, injury, death and campaign numbers remain prose.

**Strengthening:** use the companion workbook as the initial calibration
ledger. Every numeric value is marked:

- observed;
- research-bounded;
- design target;
- provisional tuning seed;
- telemetry-calibrated;
- Director accepted.

### G08 — Current first-night numbers contradict later canon

The prototype starts at 18:15 with time scale 36, begins night at 19:00, grants
a 72% shelter reduction through proximity, makes one wood unit last roughly
11.6 real seconds and raises warmth rapidly near fire. Later canon requires
150–180 seconds before serious cold, roughly 40–50% starter-refuge reduction,
60–90 seconds of useful fuel and a continuous thermal balance.

**Strengthening:** retain current behavior only as legacy vertical-slice
evidence. Replace it as one integrated Water Run calibration, not with isolated
constant changes.

### G09 — Real-device acceptance is still missing

The repository audit remains **NOT FULLY ACCEPTED**. Web touch emulation is not
Android hardware evidence. Performance, thermal behavior, gesture routing,
latency and physical readability remain unknown.

**Strengthening:** v0.1.2 remains the gate. No canon expansion changes that.

### G10 — Scope exceeds proven production capacity

The corpus can support several games. A small team cannot build all animals,
crafts, endings, disasters, skills, NPC society, multiplayer, ocean systems and
advanced technology concurrently while preserving quality on Web and Android.

**Strengthening:** representative-first production. One tool family, one
injury, one water operation, one wreck, one boat state sequence, one knowledge
carrier and one return route must carry the architecture before breadth.

### G11 — No telemetry-to-ruling loop

Acceptance checks exist, but not a unified data loop for:

- time-to-understand;
- attempts per discovery;
- abandonment and backtracking;
- dominant pressure overlap;
- inventory dwell;
- construction corrections;
- expedition return margin;
- death causes and warnings perceived;
- successor chore compression;
- device performance.

**Strengthening:** each slice declares its questions, minimal telemetry,
playtest protocol, failure thresholds and resulting tuning decision.

### G12 — Onboarding is caught between realism and silence

Recipe-free learning must not become instruction-free learning. A crash survivor
can recognize ordinary object functions without possessing manufacture-ready
patterns.

**Strengthening:** teach through:

- body sensation;
- object inspection language;
- environmental demonstration;
- visible prior use;
- comparison;
- partial personal experience;
- recoverable low-cost experiments;
- a journal that records evidence, not objectives.

## P1 — Major integration weakness

### G13 — World scale has no route budget

“Make the island bigger” is not a design number. Island area, gradients,
visibility, walking time, boat distance, safe pockets, hauling cost, landmark
density, shore sectors and wreck approach routes must be calculated together.

**Strengthening:** author travel bands:

- immediate safe pocket: under one minute while impaired;
- first loose-resource field: visible or inferable from arrival;
- competing shelter sites: meaningful trade-off, not hidden correctness;
- water route: long enough to require preparation, short enough for first
  morning;
- boatyard: a logistics commitment;
- sea and dive sites: reserve-aware expeditions with readable abort points.

### G14 — Wreckfall cadence is unbalanced

Wreck states are well designed, but arrival frequency, concurrency, probability
of useful classes, environmental load, telegraphing, persistence and missed
opportunity recovery are not calibrated.

**Strengthening:** a Wreck Director must budget:

- active major operations;
- shoreline clutter;
- guaranteed capability opportunity versus uncertain form;
- time-to-submerge or become inaccessible from physical state;
- route and contamination consequences;
- no pity drops and no campaign dead ends.

### G15 — Salvage needs a playable grammar

Inspect, stabilize, access, detach, transport, preserve and test are excellent
operations on paper. Their exact first-person interactions, time compression,
cooperative possibilities and repetition limits are unproven.

**Strengthening:** prototype one floating compartment and one forest wreck
before authoring a wreck catalogue.

### G16 — Boat progression lacks measured boat feel

The seven-state fishing boat is a signature structure, but displacement,
freeboard, cargo center, steering, paddling, sailing, motor response, beaching,
anchoring, reboarding, towing and motion comfort are uncalculated.

**Strengthening:** a Maritime Lab must prove launch, boarding, load,
propulsion, steering, capsize, reboarding and fallback before the boat carries
campaign-critical content.

### G17 — The first return route is not back-chained into the new invention law

Borrowed Signal predates the strict empty-book and physical-knowledge rules.
Its beacon repair, power, antenna, authentication and boundary crossing must be
re-mapped through tools, evidence, workspaces, tests and possible substitutions.

**Strengthening:** make the route a graph in the dependency workbook and prove
at least two solutions for every loss-sensitive critical gate.

### G18 — Permanent death lacks a complete technical transaction

The fiction is strong; implementation ownership is incomplete. Death, save
commit, disconnect, browser storage loss, cloud conflict, shared-world
authority, recovery from technical corruption and restoration policy cannot be
improvised later.

**Strengthening:** death is a server/local-authority transaction with:

- causal confirmation;
- suspension during uncertain technical state;
- atomic survivor closure;
- immutable memorial record;
- island-state preservation;
- new-arrival creation only after closure succeeds;
- technical restoration that never becomes gameplay resurrection.

### G19 — Successor openings may collapse into chores

Persistent infrastructure can either create history or turn every life into
walking between pre-solved stations.

**Strengthening:** each successor must face:

- a different body and arrival interference;
- inherited help with condition and access limits;
- at least one unresolved or newly changed operation;
- compressed solved work;
- requalification through evidence;
- a new decision created by the previous person’s life.

### G20 — The Triangle still needs a private truth ledger

Anomaly can challenge the survivor’s models but may not excuse contradiction.

**Strengthening:** production must privately define:

- invariant laws;
- translation behavior;
- time and era relations;
- what wreckfall can transport;
- boundary conditions;
- which evidence is reliable;
- which questions remain intentionally unanswered;
- forbidden explanations.

### G21 — Art and sound have no owning constitution

The game’s identity is currently carried by prose. Placeholder geometry and
synthesized tones cannot prove beauty, dread, material readability, distance,
weather, wreck scale or attachment to home.

**Strengthening:** build a visual and acoustic language for:

- material properties;
- time and weather;
- safe versus merely enclosed space;
- salvage windows;
- mechanical condition;
- human absence/presence;
- island memory;
- the boundary.

### G22 — Complex UI conflicts with the mobile promise

Inventory, Vitals and Skills are acceptable backpack tabs, but combining,
inspection, work surfaces, journal evidence, body treatment, boat systems and
return planning can overwhelm a phone screen.

**Strengthening:** use context and progressive disclosure. The world surface
owns work; the backpack owns carried matter and personal state; physical
boards/journals own accumulated plans. No universal dashboard exposes the
entire simulation.

### G23 — Anti-grind rules need measurable limits

“Repetition teaches less” is correct but not enough. The game needs similarity
keys, novelty decay, evidence thresholds, mastery variance tests and alternate
routes.

**Strengthening:** advancement rewards:

- changed material;
- changed condition;
- more difficult tolerance;
- new diagnosis;
- lower waste;
- safer method;
- successful transfer;
- documented variation.

Exact repetition primarily improves execution consistency until it saturates.

### G24 — Peace and beauty are under-budgeted

The pressure system is sophisticated; calm remains a sentence.

**Strengthening:** every campaign band requires protected intervals where the
player can repair, arrange, observe weather, improve the home, remember a
person, experiment safely or enjoy movement without a fresh emergency.

## P2 — Important later ownership

### G25 — Community Island is still a product promise

Its social verbs and constitutional purpose exist, but population, schedules,
trust, conflict, trade value, teaching, law, death authority, grief, sabotage
and offline time remain too large for immediate multiplayer.

**Strengthening:** prove a small authored solo population first. Multiplayer
later amplifies a functioning society; it does not create one by necessity.

### G26 — Ecology, hunting and agriculture need one population model

The content is rich but cross-domain equations are unfinished. Plants, soil,
water, pests, prey, waste, weather and harvest pressure must share carrying
capacity and recovery logic.

**Strengthening:** start with one edible plant, one pest, one small prey/fishing
relation and one soil/water chain. Expand only after the model produces
readable consequences rather than hidden ecological punishment.

# 6. The quantitative constitution

## 6.1 Use causal units, not false clinical precision

Player-facing bars may remain normalized, but simulation records should retain
distinct causes:

- current reserve;
- trend;
- debt;
- impairment;
- recovery opportunity;
- delayed consequence.

“Warmth 52” is a readable summary, not a literal percentage of body
temperature. “Health 78” is not “22% dead.”

## 6.2 One activity declaration

Every activity submits:

```text
duration
metabolic/intensity band
posture and force pattern
load mass, bulk, balance and hand occupation
terrain and motion
ambient, wind, rain and wetness
tool efficiency, fit and condition
skill and demonstrated technique
current fatigue, pain and impairment
novelty and feedback quality
hazard exposure
work objective and tolerance
```

The resolver returns:

```text
immediate stamina change
thermal and wetness change
hydration pressure
delayed energy/nutrition demand
pain/injury risk and warning
work progress and material transformation
tool wear
learning evidence
adaptation stimulus
recovery debt
time passage
```

No subsystem independently charges the same work again.

## 6.3 Activity seed model

The companion workbook uses an auditable seed model:

```text
effective intensity
  = base intensity
  × load modifier
  × technique inefficiency
  ÷ tool efficiency

energy demand
  = effective intensity × body mass × duration conversion

hydration demand
  = baseline time demand
  + exertion demand × heat modifier

work output
  = time × tool efficiency × skill control × readiness

learning stimulus
  = log(time)
  × novelty
  × available headroom
  × feedback quality

injury probability
  = hazard exposure
  × duration
  × fatigue/load/skill/tool modifiers
```

These are tuning relationships, not claims of medical diagnosis. Their purpose
is to force consistent direction and make assumptions visible.

## 6.4 Pressure budget

At any ordinary moment:

- one pressure may dominate;
- one may complicate;
- others should remain background or forecast;
- a lethal cascade requires either severity, accumulated neglect or repeated
  causal failure.

The balance model must flag:

- three or more simultaneous urgent pressures;
- loss of all viable recovery routes;
- failure caused mainly by interface time;
- required work whose cost exceeds the reserve needed to return;
- a new player killed before receiving readable evidence.

## 6.5 Death-spiral breaker

The design does not guarantee survival. It guarantees causal agency before
ordinary death.

Every fatal chain should normally pass through:

1. sensory change;
2. operational warning;
3. impaired performance;
4. explicit danger;
5. critical condition;
6. death if not reversed.

The player should usually retain at least two of:

- abort;
- shelter;
- unload;
- change method;
- consume reserve;
- accept lesser loss;
- call/seek help;
- leave the object;
- retreat to a prepared cache.

## 6.6 Resource and time budgets

Each campaign gate receives:

- minimum and expected inputs;
- substitution classes;
- minimum tool ancestry;
- work minutes by novice and practiced survivor;
- travel time and carried load;
- expected wear and loss;
- reserve needed for retreat;
- first-attempt failure cost;
- guaranteed evidence after failure;
- outputs needed by later gates.

No late endpoint is approved until its back-chain reaches:

- natural or wreck-fed sources;
- reachable locations;
- earlier tools/workspaces;
- the bodily capacity to perform the operations;
- discoverable evidence;
- at least one viable recovery from a lost critical component.

# 7. Competitive benchmark map

The benchmark uses leaders by capability, not one “best survival game.”

Scoring separates:

- **Canon maturity:** quality and completeness of our designed system;
- **Build maturity:** how much exists in the inspected Godot project;
- **Evidence maturity:** how strongly real player/device behavior validates it.

## 7.2 What to adopt—and what not to copy

### From The Long Dark

Adopt:

- time as a resource;
- readable environmental consequence;
- actions with duration and cost;
- route planning;
- genuine relief in safe places.

Do not copy:

- dependence on a broad premade-loot economy;
- meters behaving as independent countdown timers.

Official description: [The Long Dark Survival Mode](https://www.thelongdark.com/survival-mode/)

### From Green Hell

Adopt:

- inspectable body state;
- localized wounds and treatments;
- physical and psychological state visibly affecting play.

Do not copy:

- turning realism into icon maintenance;
- obscure ailments whose correct response requires external lookup.

Official description: [Green Hell](https://greenhell-game.com/)

### From Vintage Story

Adopt:

- material-specific work;
- technology ancestry;
- processed matter retaining meaningful state;
- civilization emerging from primitive operations.

Do not copy:

- repeating the same manual micro-operation after mastery;
- requiring pixel precision that is hostile to touch or motor accessibility.

Official description: [Vintage Story](https://www.vintagestory.at/)

### From Project Zomboid

Adopt:

- long-world sustainability;
- stations and professions crossing;
- death as a life story;
- condition, panic and skill affecting action;
- a world that continues after the first loot phase.

Do not copy:

- XP grind from low-information repetition;
- recipe-panel growth that exposes implausible knowledge;
- content breadth before the representative verbs feel good.

Official development references:
[Build 42.20 overview](https://projectzomboid.com/blog/features-overview-build-42-20/),
[Crafting RamblZ](https://projectzomboid.com/blog/news/2023/04/crafting-ramblz/)

### From Valheim

Adopt:

- home as warmth, identity and preparation;
- building that supports expression;
- sailing as a meaningful transition;
- expeditions returning with visible home upgrades.

Do not copy:

- boss keys as universal progression gates;
- biome tiers that make earlier materials mechanically obsolete.

Official description: [Valheim](https://www.valheimgame.com/)

### From Rust

Adopt:

- strong reasons to leave a secure base;
- physical landmarks and high-value operations;
- clear building territory and object state;
- social uncertainty for Community Island.

Do not copy:

- forced PvP as the primary challenge engine;
- blueprint/tech-tree unlocking as our knowledge model;
- wipes as the solution to exhausted worlds.

Official progression references:
[Meta Shift](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/meta-shift),
[Pivot or Die](https://rust.facepunch.com/news/pivot-or-die)

### From Subnautica

Adopt:

- beauty and fear sharing the same route;
- authored biomes;
- signals that suggest rather than command;
- base as expedition anchor;
- traversal capability changing reachable space.

Do not copy:

- scanning as the dominant invention rule;

# 10. Decisions that actually require the Director

No broad concept workshop is required now. The following later decisions are
genuine direction choices:

1. **Campaign life cadence**  
   Should a successful first campaign usually involve approximately 2–4
   castaways, 5–8, or highly variable lives? This determines how often the
   legacy system is experienced without turning death into a resource.

2. **Primary campaign length**  
   The design can support a focused 20–30-hour route, a 50–80-hour deep
   campaign, or an open-ended survival world with a reachable return. Production
   and pacing differ radically.

3. **Anomaly disclosure level**  
   Should a completed campaign reveal a coherent underlying mechanism, reveal
   only stable laws, or preserve several compatible explanations?

4. **Final emotional emphasis**  
   Is the strongest canonical ending expected to favor reunion, chosen island
   home, responsibility to successors, or deliberately refuse a privileged
   answer?

Everything else in the next stages is design, engineering, tuning and
playtesting work that can proceed autonomously.

# 11. Automatic whole-game failure conditions

The project fails this constitution if:

- another large catalogue is added before the current interaction gate passes;
- a system is implemented without a System Contract;
- a required endpoint is unreachable in the dependency data;
- a rare critical part can be deleted by a hidden failed-craft roll;
- the game reveals manufacture-ready recipes without proof;
- random pairing is the rational way to discover;
- body costs are charged separately by several systems;
- routine activity creates three simultaneous emergencies;
- sleep, shelter or UI transitions fabricate warmth or health;
- carrying weight is represented only by slower movement;
- an inherited station grants undocumented personal knowledge;
- a successor repeats solved chores without a changed decision;
- a missed wreck removes all viable return routes;
- the boat becomes campaign-critical before capsize and reboarding are safe;
- the Triangle explains a contradiction created by production;
- Community Island depends on live multiplayer to feel populated;
- departure triggers “home” without reception and Human Thread resolution;
- automated evidence is reported as physical acceptance;
- mobile interaction is designed after the desktop system is complete;
- art and sound remain postponed until after systems require their feedback;
- realism adds work but no meaningful choice;
- a feature cannot state what it contributes to Anchor → Read → Prepare →
  Cross → Adapt → Recover → Integrate → Choose.

# 12. Final ruling

THE FIRST NIGHT is not missing another major fantasy. It is missing the
operating constitution that makes its existing fantasies touch.

Its competitive opportunity is real:

- deeper causal making than conventional recipe survival games;
- more meaningful legacy than ordinary permadeath;
- more physical salvage than loot-based wreck exploration;
- a home that records human work rather than functioning as storage;
- a route home that continues beyond “escape.”

Its competitive deficit is also real:

- almost none of that exists in the playable build;
- the present controls and presentation have not passed the Director’s device;
- most balance relations are not yet data;
- no complete campaign proves that the depth is enjoyable.

The governing production law is:

> **Link before expanding. Calculate before multiplying. Prototype before
> cataloguing. Accept with human hands before claiming completion. Every system
> must change the body, the world, the home, the expedition or the road
> home—and preferably more than one—without hiding its cost or its cause.**

The governing player promise is:

> **Let me arrive wounded but capable. Let me understand matter by touching it.
> Let every tool remember its ancestry. Let every expedition return with
> change. Let failure leave evidence. Let death leave a world. Let the island
> become worth saving. Then make leaving it a real journey and a difficult
> human choice.**
